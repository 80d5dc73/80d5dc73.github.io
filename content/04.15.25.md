### Power
For some reason, I'm craving power. I want the power of ownership, success, traveling around on a corporate jet, making myself feel important. Though, I feel that as a Christian, I cannot have that. Why God? Why can't I have both? 

My fear is that even asking questions (and thinking these thoughts) is a complete insult to God. I know that is not the case. 

I fear that God is a governor in my life. I think that's a common thing that people worry about. However, as I've learned from Dallas Willard, if I've learning anything at all, the reflection of your actions an thoughts are a direct reflection of your spiritual reformation. Essentially, I need to reform myself, and I feel these questions will be answered. 

I don't think it's even about money. Is it? I really just want to feel this sense of self-importance. That the work I'm doing is actually changing the world at a large scale. To do something that people find value in. I know that this is the entire point of being a Christian, so it feels that I'm replacing that with this dream for power. Can I really not have both? 

I really can only answer these questions through spiritual formation. Everyone's walk with God is different...

I'm just extremely confident that I can continually perform under pressure. 

### Callused
I have had a few conversations lately that made me realize how callused I am. I'm not sure what it was, probably a mix of things: 

1. I probably cried less than three times (at any point) during my parent's divorce. I can only remember one time.
2. I lost it the first time I had an offer rescinded. The second time, I barely even though about it. I just continued with life. 
   
Sure, I can attribute a lot of that to emotional maturity, but there's some things I really should deal with. 

I can't share my winnings or my losses with anyone except for my mom and my girlfriend. Hell, I just started to share my wins and losses with my girlfriend. 

I just feel that no one else deserves to be bogged down with my problems and, consequently, it'd be dishonest to only show them the highs. Plus, I generally feel that expressing my highs is out of a sense of pride. Ironically, even though I've been dreaming of power, I'd say that I'm pretty good at keeping my pride in check...

### Lust
Lord, it's not an easy battle. Though, I'm so proud of how far I've come! 4 weeks clean. Obviously, there have been a few run-ins and I'm not idyllically perfect, but as far as I'm counting, I am. 

I really wish that I didn't have the natural masculine pull to check out other women. Honestly, even my girlfriend. As the Bible says, to even look at a woman with lust is to commit adultery in your mind. 

Even though I'm essentially certain that I'll be marrying my girlfriend, I still want to be so careful of that adulterous mindset. 

I'm called to be stronger than that.

If I was truly undergoing spiritual transformation, I don't think it'd even be something I'd struggle with. 

I sometimes fantasize about what we can over the summer, but, I know, that I can't do these things. I'm called to be of higher character. The flesh is just too fervorous. 

How can I expect to be spiritually transformed until it's no longer a yearn? Is that even possible?

I don't doubt my faith. Even. But sometimes, my sinful being really wishes I could.

All of this said, nothing detracts from the Lord. I place my full confidence and trust in Him and will strive to be as similar to Jesus as possible.
